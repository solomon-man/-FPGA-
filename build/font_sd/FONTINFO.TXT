FPGA Chinese font resource
FONT16.BIN: packed 16x16 monochrome glyphs, row-major, MSB is left pixel.
FONT16.MEM: one hexadecimal byte per line, suitable as a BRAM init source.
FONTMAP.TXT: glyph index and UTF-8 character mapping.
Glyphs: 56; bytes per glyph: 32; total bytes: 1792.
The FPGA initializes its BRAM font during configuration from font16_rows.mem. SD files are resource copies, not loaded at runtime.
